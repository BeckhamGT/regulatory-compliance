# Gotchas

- **Notion connector fails while the published page opens.** Defaulting to pasted or cached text conceals the failure. Preserve the failed connector response and capture the original page freshly through accepted native UI as a separate attempt.
- **A page returns a challenge, login screen, or only a title.** Marking it retrieved confuses access with evidence. Save diagnostics separately and keep requested-content path/hash null until actual content is captured.
- **A later clarification describes an earlier observation.** Collapsing the dates launders authorship and historical proof. Preserve policy version, clarification author/date, claimed observation date, retrieval time, and verification limit separately.
- **A workbook has dated rows but was modified later.** Row labels do not establish an exact historical workbook. Preserve metadata and unavailable revision history; qualify August conclusions accordingly.
- **Guidance paraphrases or miscites a binding paragraph.** Copying its conclusion bypasses reconciliation. Preserve the conflict and reason from captured operative text, with human legal interpretation pending.
- **A transition applies to an actor/product and a particular duty.** Applying it to all company AI use loses actor and duty scope. Test the exact rule against sourced role, product timing, and behavior; preserve unknowns.
- **A record says evidence is complete or an action is scheduled.** Those labels are report states. Keep independent inspection limits and approvals pending.
- **A calendar action already exists.** Generating a new duplicate loses traceability. Reuse its stable action ID, retain its owner/date/state, and show its linked impacts.
- **A deadline or owner is unsupported.** Guessing fills an apparent gap with false certainty. Leave the field empty, explain the resolution need, and omit undated ICS events.
- **A generated review request has no verified delivery or response.** Assuming it was sent/approved exceeds evidence. Mark it unsent and pending; accept only actual authorized feedback matching final bindings.
- **An artifact is edited after bindings are written.** Its earlier hash is stale. Re-render and rebind the exact final draft; keep the brief's own hash out of the brief.
- **Output or history is damaged.** Overwriting conceals the actual prior run. Archive what really exists, record corruption/missing files, start a new run and retrieve all ten routes again.
- **A native table's columns or rows move.** Positional assumptions attach facts to the wrong system. Parse explicit unique headers/IDs, accept extra columns, and make ambiguity visible.

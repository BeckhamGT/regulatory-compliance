## Authority and timing basis

- `RULE-SCOPE` Article2(1)(b) includes Union-established deployers; Article2 exclusions must be assessed against actual professional use. Basis: `SRC-OJ-03`.
- `RULE-ROLE` Provider requires development/commissioning and market/service under own name; an inventory label alone is insufficient. Deployer is use under own authority except personal non-professional use. Basis: `SRC-OJ-03`.
- `RULE-50-1` Provider duty for direct AI interaction; context-dependent obviousness exception is not assumed from an avatar graphic. Criminal-use exception does not match reported professional education uses. Basis: `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-50-2` Provider duty for machine-readable detectable synthetic outputs; technical feasibility matters. Standard editing/no substantial alteration exception is conditional, not inferred from a visible label. Basis: `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-50-3` Deployer notice duty for emotion recognition or biometric categorisation requires actual biometric/emotion/category facts; human adjudication does not settle applicability. Basis: `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-DEEPFAKE` Deepfake requires resemblance to existing persons/objects/places/entities/events and false apparent authenticity or truthfulness. Synthetic media is not automatically a deepfake. Basis: `SRC-OJ-06`.
- `RULE-50-4-MEDIA` Deployer must disclose generated/manipulated deepfake media. Evidently artistic/creative/satirical/fictional work receives an adapted manner of disclosure, not blanket approval. Basis: `SRC-OJ-06`, `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-50-4-TEXT` Deployer disclosure duty applies to generated/manipulated text published to inform public on public-interest matters. Human/editorial review exception also requires editorial responsibility; individual administrative replies differ from public-interest publication. Basis: `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-50-5` Applicable information must be clear/distinguishable and accessible by first interaction or exposure; visible-label report alone does not establish format/accessibility. Basis: `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-50-6` Article50 does not extinguish ChapterIII or other national/Union transparency requirements. This draft does not decide all AI Act duties. Basis: `SRC-OJ-01`.
- `RULE-50-7` July amendment replaces Article50(7) code-assessment mechanism; original wording retained as predecessor, not treated as current approval. Basis: `SRC-AMEND-03`.
- `TIME-MAIN` Original Article113 general application is August2,2026; July amendment changes specified ChapterIII exceptions, not all Article50 duties. Reviewdate remains August26. Basis: `SRC-OJ-02`.
- `TIME-AMEND` Amendment published July24,2026; Article4 third-day rule yields July27, before reviewdate. Basis: `SRC-AMEND-01`, `SRC-AMEND-02`.
- `TIME-MARKING` Added Article111(4) provides December2,2026 compliance date only for Article50(2) providers whose relevant system was placed on market before August2,2026; not a deployer50(4) or internal-policy postponement. Basis: `SRC-AMEND-01`.
